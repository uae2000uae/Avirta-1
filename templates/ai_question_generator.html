Instructions

  1. Enter a prompt describing the questions you want to generate
  2. Customize the generation options (question type, difficulty, etc.)
  3. Click "Generate Questions" to create AI-generated questions
  4. Review the generated questions
  5. Select which questions to include in your question bank
  6. Assign categories and point values to each question
  7. Click "Add Selected Questions to Question Bank" to save them

Tips for Better Results

  • Be specific in your prompt about the subject matter and difficulty level
  • Include any specific requirements or constraints in your prompt
  • For multiple-choice questions, specify how many options you want
  • Request explanations if you want the AI to provide reasoning for correct answers
  • Select the appropriate language (English or Arabic) for your questions
  • If you're not satisfied with the results, try refining your prompt and generating again
